## Design Context

- Audience: one person checking email on their own computer (PRD).
- Purpose: prepare local setup and select Gmail, pasted text, or an imported email.
- Direction: calm, minimal, light; confirmed by owner October 25, 2026.
- Voice: calm, clear, careful. No reassurance unsupported by evidence.
- Use local fonts, restrained green accents, generous spacing, and visible focus.
- Keep setup status explicit, all controls keyboard accessible, and layout usable
  on narrow screens. No external fonts, images, or runtime asset requests.
